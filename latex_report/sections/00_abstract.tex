\begin{abstract}
We forecast the 24 hourly price changes of Bitcoin's next day from
hourly Bitstamp data (2020--2026). An exploratory analysis on the
training years shows that the direction of hourly moves is almost noise,
while their size is persistent, follows the clock and drifts over time;
these findings shape the features, targets and models. With chronological
blocks, choices made without the test block and comparison against
simple rules, no model (linear, trees, neural networks or the zero-shot
foundation model Timestral) beats a forecast of ``no change'' for the
returns, apart from a small reversal one hour ahead (AUC 0.54). The size
of each hourly move, however, is predictable: monthly-refitted Gradient
Boosting reaches $R^2=0.148$, and its average with Timestral 0.164,
against 0.056 for a volatility rule; recalibrated 80\% ranges keep 80\%
coverage. In a trading simulation these forecasts do not earn more money
but make trading safer.
\end{abstract}
